Biography

Zheng Lin is currently a third-year PhD student at the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, The University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Xianhao Chen and Prof. Kaibin Huang. Before that, he received his M.E. degree from Fudan University, advised by Prof. Zhe Chen and Prof. Yue Gao, and his B.E. degree from Fuzhou University in 2020.
