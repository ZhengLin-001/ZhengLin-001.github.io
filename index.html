Biography

Zheng Lin will join the Interdisciplinary Centre for Security, Reliability and Trust (SnT), University of Luxembourg, as a Postdoctoral Researcher in August 2026, working with Prof. Symeon Chatzinotas. He received his Ph.D. degree from The University of Hong Kong in 2026, supervised by Prof. Xianhao Chen and co-supervised by Prof. Kaibin Huang. Before that, he received the M.E. degree from Fudan University in 2023, supervised by Prof. Yue Gao and co-supervised by Prof. Zhe Chen, and B.E. degree from Fuzhou University in 2020. Furthermore, he works closely with Prof. Wei Ni and Prof. Jun Luo.

I am always open to exploring new ideas and actively welcome academic collaborations. Beyond research, I genuinely enjoy connecting with fellow scholars and making new friends. Please feel free to reach out to me via email: zhenglin@ieee.org. Thank you very much!
