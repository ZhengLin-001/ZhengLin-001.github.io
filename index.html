Biography

Zheng Lin is currently a third-year PhD student at the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, The University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Xianhao Chen and Prof. Kaibin Huang. Before that, he received his M.E. degree from Fudan University, advised by Prof. Zhe Chen and Prof. Yue Gao, and B.E. degree from Fuzhou University in 2020.

[Collaboration] I enjoy making friends! If you are interested in fostering a friendship or collaborating with me, please feel free to reach out (wechat: lz891945739 or email: linzheng@eee.hku.hk / zhenglin0425a@163.com). I look forward to hearing from you!
